As a Teaching Assistant for the Statistical Machine Learning course (IE343) at KAIST, I support undergraduate students in developing a strong foundation in machine learning concepts and techniques.

Responsibilities
======
* Grade assignments and provide detailed feedback on student work
* Hold regular office hours to assist students with course material

Course Topics
======
* Supervised and unsupervised learning
* Regression and classification techniques
* Dimensionality reduction
* Model evaluation and validation

This role allows me to strengthen my own understanding of statistical machine learning principles while helping students develop the skills needed to apply these concepts to real-world problems.
